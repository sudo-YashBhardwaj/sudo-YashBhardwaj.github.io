Yash Bhardwaj

I am a master's student in Trustworthy and Responsible AI at École Polytechnique. From April to September 2026 I was a research intern in the Willow team at Inria Paris, advised by Cordelia Schmid and Shizhe Chen, pretraining an object-centric 3D encoder that turns a fused point cloud into a fixed set of object tokens, each grounded by an instance mask and carrying the object's class, part structure and articulation, and plugging those tokens into vision-language-action manipulation policies in place of dense point features. Before that, at Georgia Tech's Financial Services Innovation Lab, I co-authored two benchmarks of what multimodal LLMs understand in video, at KDD 2025 (oral) and an ICCV 2025 workshop. I came to research from engineering: three and a half years as a software engineer at Urban Company, building production data and ML systems.

I am looking for research internships starting April 2027 in robot learning and embodied AI, multimodal learning, diffusion and flow matching, and LLM and VLM pre- and post-training.
