Yash Bhardwaj

I am a master's student in AI at École Polytechnique, interested in robot learning, 3D perception and multimodal models. Most recently I was a research intern at Inria Paris in the Willow team, with Cordelia Schmid and Shizhe Chen, on pretraining 3D representations for vision-language-action models and downstream manipulation. Before that I worked with Georgia Tech on multimodal AI for finance and co-authored two papers in the area, at KDD 2025 (oral) and an ICCV 2025 workshop. I came to research after three and a half years as a software engineer at Urban Company.

Looking for research internships from April 2027 in robot learning, multimodal learning, diffusion and flow matching, and LLM and VLM pre- and post-training.
