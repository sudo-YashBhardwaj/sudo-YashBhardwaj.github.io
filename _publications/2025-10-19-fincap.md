**Problem.** Short-form financial videos carry much of their meaning visually — charts, gestures, on-screen tickers, emotion — yet it is unclear how much each modality contributes when an MLLM is asked to describe them.

**Contribution.** A topic-aligned captioning benchmark on 624 annotated YouTube short videos, evaluating every modality combination (T, A, V, TA, TV, AV, TAV) across five topics: main recommendation, sentiment, video purpose, visual analysis, and financial entity recognition. Captions are scored with G-VEval and with F1 on ticker–action pairs.

**Results.**
- Video alone performs strongly on four of five topics, underscoring the value of visual context and cues such as emotion, gesture and body language.
- Selective pairs (TV, AV) often outperform the full TAV input: adding modalities can add noise rather than signal.
- Best scores: 0.60 F1 on ticker–action extraction and 85% accuracy on main recommendation (Gemini 2.0 Flash, AV); 92% on visual analysis (GPT-5 mini, V).
